## How to use Bestie

Bestie is built and trained on **Claude**.

**Claude.ai (free or paid):** open [`SKILL.md`](skills/bestie/SKILL.md), click Copy raw file, then paste it into a new chat, or into a Project's instructions so she is always on. Then brain dump.

**Claude Code:** copy the `skills/bestie` folder into `.claude/skills/` in your project. Bestie loads whenever you brain dump.

**Claude Console:** paste `SKILL.md` into the system prompt in the Workbench.

See [`EXAMPLE.md`](skills/bestie/EXAMPLE.md) for a real brain dump and her answer.

## Train your Bestie

She only gets good if you train her. Tell her your decisions and rules, correct her out loud, give her a cap, and let her say no. A Chief of Staff who agrees with everything is a mirror.
