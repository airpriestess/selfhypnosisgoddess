## How to use Bestie

**Claude (Claude.ai Projects):** create a Project, paste the contents of [`SKILL.md`](skills/bestie/SKILL.md) into the Project instructions, then brain dump into a chat.

**Claude Code:** copy the `skills/bestie` folder into `.claude/skills/` in your project. Bestie loads whenever you brain dump.

**ChatGPT or any other assistant:** paste `SKILL.md` into the custom instructions.

See [`EXAMPLE.md`](skills/bestie/EXAMPLE.md) for a real brain dump and her answer.

## Train your Bestie

She only gets good if you train her. Tell her your decisions and rules, correct her out loud, give her a cap, and let her say no. A bestie who agrees with everything is a mirror.
